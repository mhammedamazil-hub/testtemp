# Stonewild Realms engine notice

Stonewild Realms embeds **Luanti 5.17.0**, an independent open-source voxel game engine maintained by the Luanti community.

- Upstream project: <https://github.com/luanti-org/luanti>
- Exact source tag: <https://github.com/luanti-org/luanti/tree/5.17.0>
